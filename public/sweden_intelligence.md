## Tick ​​season is here - how to protect the children and yourself
**Outlet:** TV4 Nyheterna
**Category:** General News | **Date:** 2026-04-27 (today)

Ticks come to life already at five plus degrees and are now found all over the country, even in northern Sweden. This means that the risk of contracting TBE or Lyme disease is almost everywhere we spend time outdoors. TBE is a viral disease that can affect the brain and for which vaccination is recommended, while Lyme disease is a bacterial infection that can be treated with antibiotics. Full-covering clothing and skin inspection The most important protection against ticks is to wear full-covering clothing, especially for children who often play in grass and bushes. Thin trousers and pulled up socks reduce the risk of ticks attaching to the skin. Bright clothes also make it easier to spot the little crawlers. - We cover the children from the sun, but we also need to think about ticks. Inspect the skin regularly, especially where the skin is thin such as in the groin, knee folds and behind the ears, says Johan Holmgren. Ticks thrive where it's warm and humid, and can crawl around for a while before biting. Therefore, it is important to look extra carefully at the whole body after going out, not least at small children. How to remove a tick Although tick bites can feel worrying, the vast majority are harmless. But if you discover a tick, it is important to remove it as quickly as possible, just to be safe. Tweezers work great - grab the tick as close to the skin as possible and pull straight out. Special tools exist, but the most important thing is that you feel comfortable with what you use. - Removing a tick is not something we need to seek treatment for. We do that ourselves at home, says Johan Holmgren. Then wash the skin with soap and water. If you develop symptoms such as fever, headache or a rash that spreads, you should contact the health service, as this may indicate TBE or Lyme disease.
