Not all refrigerators are the same Modern refrigerators often have circulating cold air, which makes the temperature more even. How to place different foods Dairy products, seafood and other sensitive food should be in the middle of the fridge, where the temperature is lowest. The door compartment is the warmest part of the refrigerator and is best suited for products that are not so sensitive, such as ketchup, mayonnaise and some dressings. Milk and dairy products should not be left there, as they turn sour faster. - In the door compartments, you should have things that are not so sensitive, she says. Tips to avoid food waste Eggs keep best in the refrigerator and can be stored significantly longer than at room temperature. Tomatoes do best outside the fridge, while lemons can be kept in the vegetable drawer. - You don't want to throw away food unnecessarily, says Marie-Louise Danielsson Tham. The Government has today decided to provide SEK 1.4 billion in support to a new World Bank crisis facility that benefits Ukraine. This support enables new loans totalling approximately SEK 4.2 billion for the country’s reconstruction and recovery. The World Bank is a key actor for Ukraine’s reform efforts and economic resilience during Russia’s ongoing aggression. Sweden’s actions have been vital for the establishment of the new crisis facility.
